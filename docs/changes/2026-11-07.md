## Rev 3 (same day): plain-English rewrite + corrected widget routing

Two requested fixes. Vincent asked for the plain-English pass and a fact-check on the
widget's routing. The title is **unchanged** pending his A/B/C choice.

### 1. Plain-English rewrite (EN + ZH)

Measured before and after, prose only (tables and Sources excluded — the Sources
section lists paper titles and is exempt by design):

```
EN   avg sentence  22.8 words  ->  10.2 words      (target <=12)
EN   jargon terms      13      ->  0
ZH   avg sentence  26.7 chars  ->  17.6 chars      (sentences over 45 chars: 9 -> 1)
```

Jargon removed from the body: tendinopathy, pathology, pathoanatomical, modalities,
orthoses, radicular, remodel, "loading prescription", "passive care",
"musculoskeletal physiotherapist" (in reader-facing copy).

Technique that worked: one idea per sentence, and split on the conjunctions. A
sentence with an em-dash, "which", "while" or "so that" was always two sentences.
Word count was never the target — sentence shape was, exactly as the standing
writing contract says.

### 2. Widget routing — three substantive errors corrected

| # | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `q2 local + q1 timing` -> **podiatry** | removed; routes to **physio** | APTA 2023 heel-pain CPG is a *physiotherapy* guideline; physio is the correct first call. Podiatry is now named inside the physio verdict as the alternative when arch support is also needed. |
| 2 | `q4 passive` (months of passive care) selected a practitioner type | q4 now only distinguishes **stay** vs **carry on** | What treatment you have already tried does not tell you which tissue is injured. It is not a diagnostic input. |
| 3 | `q1 redflag` -> "**promptly**, today or tomorrow" | "within days rather than weeks"; "red flag" wording removed | Overtriaged. Night pain is a genuine flag but heel fat pad atrophy also causes it (PMC3309235). Days-not-weeks is accurate without alarming. |

Routes reduced **5 -> 4**: urgent, neuro, physio, stay. The podiatry route was deleted
rather than re-labelled, because with q4 removed as a selector it had no distinct
input that selected it.

⚠️ **Differential gap still open and deliberately NOT "fixed" by the widget:**
heel fat pad atrophy presents with night pain and bilateral pain, not first-step pain.
The widget does not — and should not — route on that, because it is a clinical
diagnosis. It is named in the article's closing note instead. A routing tool that
pretended to differentiate it would be inventing precision.
