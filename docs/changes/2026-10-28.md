## The defect

`grip-strength-the-mortality-number-lifters-ignore` told the reader:

> EWGSOP2 flags **grip below 27 kg in men** as probable sarcopenia…
> if a middle-aged man can't reliably close **30 kg**… that's a quiet alarm.

Both numbers are **age-blind**, and they under-warn a man in his fifties.

`EWGSOP2` (Cruz-Jentoft 2019) is the **European** consensus. The **Asian Working Group
for Sarcopenia 2025 update** publishes **age-stratified** cut-offs:

| Age | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| 50–64 | **< 34 kg** | < 20 kg |
| ≥ 65 | **< 28 kg** | < 18 kg |

Consequence: a 52-year-old closing 31 kg reads as *fine* against the 27 kg line and
*below the line* against the AWGS 50–64 cut-off. The post reassured the reader it
should have flagged.

⚠️ **The correction only widens the warning when the stricter age-specific cut-off is
the applicable one.** A reader who is not Asian, or who is over 65, is not
under-warned by the old text. The post now states both consensuses and refuses to
adjudicate between them — which threshold applies is a clinical call.
