## Evidence backbone (Stage 4b fact-check)
- **Thomas et al. 2018** (Med Sci Sports Exerc 50(12):2526-2535, PMID 30067591) —
  heavy (10×5 @80% 1RM) / jump / sprint each produced fatigue needing up to 72 h
  to resolve, BUT "not primarily underpinned by decrements in CNS function."
  Peripheral decrements 48 h; reduced voluntary activation only 24 h (jump/sprint)
  / 48 h (heavy). **This is the myth-buster.**
- **"Is the notion of central fatigue based on a solid foundation?"** (J Appl
  Physiol, 2016) — peripheral-only model reproduces force behaviour attributed to
  central factors in submaximal contractions.
- **"Quantification of central fatigue: a central debate"** (Eur J Appl Physiol,
  2021) — twitch interpolation may OVERESTIMATE central fatigue; central fatigue
  does exist but quantification is contested.
- **Addleman et al. 2024** (PMID 38921629) — HRV is a recovery signal, not a
  CNS-damage meter.
