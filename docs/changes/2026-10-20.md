## Evidence base (primary sources, all cited in-post)

- Incidence, prevalence and nature of injuries in padel: a systematic review.
  *BMJ Open Sport & Exercise Medicine* 2023;9:e001607. PMID 37337550 · PMCID PMC10277135
  → 3 injuries/1,000 training hrs, 8/1,000 matches, prevalence 40–95%,
  elbow > knee > shoulder > lower back, predominantly tendinous/muscular.
- Injuries and risk factors in Swedish padel. S0949328X22002605
  → n=457, **median age 42**, 66.1% men, **36.5% annual injury incidence**,
  **median recovery 30 days**, age >30 an independent factor, load-driven.
- Match Analysis, Physical Training, Risk of Injury and Rehabilitation in Padel.
  PMCID PMC8998509 → core stabilisation + trunk strength for unilateral
  overhead sport; grip decrements after simulated matches.
- Ball Impact Position in Recreational Male Padel Players. PMCID PMC7828082
  → technique-driven elbow load; wrist stability at contact.
- Padel related injuries in Chilean amateur players. PMCID PMC12220762
  → >36yo more likely injured in training than competition.

**Thesis:** the strongest predictor of padel injury is **load**, not age or sex.
Ramp rate is the villain. Two short strength sessions/week is the prescribed
fix — the same programme that keeps a reader independent at 70.
