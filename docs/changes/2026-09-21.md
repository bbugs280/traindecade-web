**So the re-frame was executed as two genuinely-missing EVIDENCE additions instead** — verified absent

   manufacturer of the **BeefISO™** ingredient it tested. Live SERP check (2026-09-21) confirms
   **most competitor pages cite the 2015 conference abstract**, which carries no funding statement.

   ⚠️ **Build-tester caught a real sourcing error in the first draft of this commit** (see below).
   The initial version asserted the funding statement was *in* the cited `PMC4595383`, and told
   readers to verify it there. **`PMC4595383` is a conference ABSTRACT (JISSN 12(Suppl 1):P11)** —
   it has no funding section, and "Essentia" appears zero times on it. The linked page would not
   have corroborated the claim — exactly the failure mode an evidence-validation page exists to
   prevent. **Fixed** by re-sourcing to the 2018 full paper (**PMID 28399016**, *J Strength Cond
   Res* 32(8):2233–2242), whose published funding statement reads: *"Essentia Metabolic Proteins
   provided funding for this trial and all funding went to The University of Tampa."* The venue
   change (2015 abstract → 2018 full paper) is now stated in the copy itself, so the reader can see
   which version carries the declaration.

   ⚠️ **Framed honestly, not as a "gotcha":** the text explicitly notes the null result *favours
   whey* (the funder's rival), so this is disclosure, not a bias accusation. Directionally this
   strengthens rather than weakens the page's "beef is a legitimate tie" verdict. Also corrected:
   the 2018 trial tested **whey concentrate**, where beef, chicken and whey all came out even.

## Third commit — coherence defect caught by build-tester's re-test

The build-tester re-test on `5be6697` returned **⚠️ SHIP WITH WARNINGS** with a sharp finding: **the
venue-change note contradicted the page's own body copy eight lines above it.** The body and Sources
entries still cited the study as *JISSN 2015*, while the new paragraph correctly said the full paper
was 2018 — so the page flagged a venue change as an important disclosure while still giving the wrong
venue as its primary citation. Two identical stale entries per file.

**Root cause: the fix was local where it needed to be global.** Adding an accurate note made the
pre-existing inconsistency visible; it didn't cause it.

Fixed in the third commit:
```

                             conference abstract"; "men and women ... or a control"

                             abstract listed as the abstract
· funding sentence          → quotation marks REMOVED (build-tester could not verify the verbatim
                             wording; now reads as accurate paraphrase: "funded by ... with the
                             funding going to the University of Tampa")
· Sources funding entry     → states explicitly that the funding section is in the FULL TEXT
                             (subscription) and NOT in the free abstract
· PDCAAS Sources entry      → now attributed (FAO/WHO protein-quality evaluation; beef 0.92 per
                             Schaafsma 2000) — closed build-tester's W5
```

⚠️ **Standing honesty note for future sessions:** the verbatim funding wording is **not
independently verifiable in public sources** — the full text is paywalled and PubMed's record carries
no funding field. The claim rests on the paper's declared funding plus the author affiliation
(*Applied Science and Performance Institute / University of Tampa* + *Maximum Human Performance*).
The page now says "the full text (subscription)" rather than implying the reader can check it in the
free abstract. **Do not re-add a block quote of the funding sentence without a citable public source.**
