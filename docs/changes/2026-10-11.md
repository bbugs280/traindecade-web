## ⚠️ Issue found and resolved during the build

**The stray-emphasis guard flagged BOTH new files** (`__ x18` EN, `__ x20` ZH).

**Cause:** the guard counts **any** `__` in rendered text (line 81: `text.count("__")`), and the cheat sheet used runs of underscores as fill-in blanks (`Boot size: ______ US`). These are legitimate content, not broken emphasis — a **false positive of a necessary heuristic**.

**Fix applied:** replaced all fill-in underscore runs with middle-dot blanks (`········`). No guard change — the guard protects against a real 308-stray regression and was NOT weakened.

**⚠️ Worth recording:** any future post with a fill-in form, code sample, or `__dunder__` reference will trip this guard the same way. Prefer dots/dashes for blanks rather than editing the test.
