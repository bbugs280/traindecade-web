# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 August 2026_

Train Decade ("we", "us", "our") operates traindecade.com (the "Site"). This page explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## What we collect

- **Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site (pages viewed, approximate location, device type). This helps us write better content.
- **Advertising.** We may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites.

## Cookies

We and our partners use cookies to remember preferences, measure traffic, and personalise ads. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

## Personal information

We do not collect personal information for marketing, and we never sell your data. If you contact us by email, we use your email address only to reply.

## Third parties

Google Analytics and Google AdSense are operated by Google LLC. Their use of data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

## Your choices

- **Opt out of personalised ads:** visit [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- **Opt out of analytics:** use a browser extension or the "Do Not Track" setting.
