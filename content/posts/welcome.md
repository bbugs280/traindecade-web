**Train Decade** is a fitness and body recomposition resource built around one idea: meaningful, lasting change happens over years, not weeks.

Most fitness content is optimized for clicks and sales — six-week plans, crash cuts, and before-and-after photos that quietly stop working the moment the camera turns off. Train Decade is the opposite: calm, evidence-based guidance for men who want to train and eat well for the rest of their lives, not just for the next thirty days.

## What we cover

- **Training** — strength and hypertrophy programming that survives busy schedules, injuries, and plateaus.
- **Body recomposition** — the science of losing fat and building muscle at the same time, done properly.
- **Nutrition** — sustainable eating for cut and gain phases, without the diet-industry theatrics.
- **Longevity** — the habits that keep men strong and healthy into their forties, fifties, and beyond.

## The core principle

**Consistency beats intensity.** The methods that work aren't glamorous — they're repeatable. Every article here is written to be understood, and more importantly, to be *used*, year after year.

Cut and gain aren't events; they're phases that last for months. Progress isn't measured against yesterday — it's measured against the trendline you've held for years.

## Where to start

Begin with [Why a Decade, Not a 6-Week Challenge](/posts/why-a-decade-not-a-6-week-challenge/) — it lays out the philosophy behind everything else on this site.

A decade is coming either way. Build something worth having when it arrives.
